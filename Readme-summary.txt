Atlantis Mod Summary

Overview
Atlantis is a highly specialized civilization built around a restrained empire model, volcanic settlement potential, and strong production and civic identity. Its design favors a single-city structure, efficient wonder development, and a mythic culture progression rooted in Atlantean history and legend.

Core Abilities
- Civilization Trait: Fairest of All Plains
  - Can only have one city.
  - Towns cannot be converted into cities.
  - Can work Volcano tiles.
  - Gains +30% Production toward constructing the Temple of Poseidon.

- Unique Unit: War Chariot
  - Atlantian unique War Chariot unit.
  - Includes tier 2 and tier 3 versions of the unit.

Culture and Civic Summary
- Atlantian Civics
  - Pillars of Hercules I
  - Hunt of the Bulls
  - Laws of the Ten Kings
  - Oricalicum
  - Atlantis Origins, Atlantis Renaissance, and Atlantis Modernization are part of the civilization’s culture progression path.

- Civic Effects
  - Hunt of the Bulls: Increased Culture on Rural Districts in Cities during a Golden Age; increased Gold on Rural Districts outside a Golden Age.
  - Laws of the Ten Kings: Increased Influence on Urban Districts in Cities during a Golden Age; increased Happiness on Urban Districts outside a Golden Age.

- Tradition Effects
  - Critias: Increased Culture and Science for the Palace from Quarters.
  - Sixth Portion: Increased healing for Cavalry and +100% Gold maintenance for Cavalry military units.
  - Pillars of Hercules II and III: Increased Gold from Urban Center Towns.

Summary
Atlantis is a focused, expansion-limited civilization that emphasizes production efficiency, volcanic tile exploitation, and culture-driven progression. Its playstyle rewards a centralized empire, powerful wonder construction, and strategic use of civic and tradition bonuses tied to mythic and classical Atlantean themes.
